Fertilizer and Agricultural Product Booking System

Home

Kisan Suvidha is a digital platform that helps farmers check agricultural products and book them easily.

About the Project

This project aims to make fertilizer booking easier and reduce unnecessary waiting at distribution centres.

How It Works

  1. Farmer registers on the platform.
  2. Farmer checks available products.
  3. Farmer selects the required product.
  4. Farmer places a booking.
  5. Farmer collects the product from the assigned centre.

Available Products

  • Fertilizers
  • Seeds
  • Agricultural Products
